We may process the following personal data:

We do not use your data for marketing without your explicit consent, and do not sell it to third parties.

4. Legal Basis

  • Legitimate interest — ensuring website security and operation.

5. Analytics

This website uses Yandex Metrica (Yandex LLC, Russia), a web analytics service. It collects anonymised data about visits: pages viewed, time on site, traffic source, device and browser type, and session recordings (Webvisor). Data is transmitted to Yandex servers and processed in accordance with Yandex's Privacy Policy.

Yandex Metrica uses cookies to identify returning visitors. You may opt out by installing the Yandex opt-out browser extension or by disabling cookies in your browser settings.

6. Third-Party Processors

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may process technical data (IP addresses, HTTP headers) in accordance with GitHub's Privacy Statement.

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts (Google LLC), which may receive your IP address when fonts are fetched.

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with third parties except where required by applicable law.

7. Cookies

This website uses cookies through Yandex Metrica for analytics purposes. By continuing to use the site you consent to the use of cookies. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect site functionality.

8. Data Retention

Data provided through enquiries is retained for as long as necessary to respond to your request or fulfil a contract, after which it is deleted or anonymised unless required by law to retain longer.

To exercise these rights, send a request to info@s-advisors.com. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.
