02 · What to do?

There are proven tools,
but an additional layer is needed

Business clubs

Horizontal exchange

Regular check-ins with peers — inspiration, networking, others' success stories and mistakes.

Rarely dive deep into your specific business
Consulting

Project expertise

An external team for a targeted task — strategy, M&A, transformation. Deep work, concrete results.

Expensive and context disappears once the project is delivered
Board of Directors

Quarterly oversight

Independent directors and advisory boards — an external view on major decisions, a strategic overlay.

Rarely engages with the operational rhythm
StratCouncil

Regular strategic process

External perspective, a circle of peers, and a decision-making structure — but not one-off and not once a quarter, rather once every two weeks. Without budget spikes, with accumulated context, between major strategy reviews and between one-off consulting projects.

StratCouncil is not a replacement for other working tools, but an effective complement. It fills the space that used to close on its own: through time, resilience, and intuition.
03 · What it is

StratCouncil — three elements
in one subscription

What used to require three separate budgets and three separate providers is now consolidated into one regular format.

01

Top-tier consultants on a subscription basis

The Senior Advisors team with experience on projects for Sberbank, Kaspersky, MTS, Severstal, ivi and others. Previously, access to this level of expertise required large project budgets. The subscription makes the same thing ongoing.

02

A regular strategic process

Two workshops per month, personal sessions with Denis Sobe-Panek, an ongoing chat with the StratCouncil team. Strategic decisions are reviewed and adjusted every two weeks — not once every 3–5 years.

03

A circle of peers

Up to ten participants per cohort. C-level executives of companies with revenue from ₽500 million. Testing hypotheses not with subordinates, not with investors, not with competitors — but with peers.
